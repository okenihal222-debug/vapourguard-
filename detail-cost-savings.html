Where The Savings Actually Come From

Where the number on the dashboard actually comes from, and what it looks like for a station like yours.

Not a rebate, not an incentive

The saving isn't a subsidy or a marketing bonus — it's fuel the station already purchased, that would otherwise have evaporated and never been sold. Capturing it and returning it to sellable inventory is the entire mechanism. There's no new revenue stream to build and no new customer behaviour required.

What the base case looks like

Using a base-case model — 6,000 L/day station throughput, 0.30% evaporation loss, 75% assumed capture efficiency, ₹98.5/L blended fuel price — that works out to roughly 13.5 L recovered per day, or about ₹4.85 lakh per year, against a ₹2,00,000 assumed hardware cost.
