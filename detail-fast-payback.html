What 'Fast' Means In This Model

What 'fast' actually means once you plug real assumptions into the model.

Payback scales with what a station actually sells

Because the savings are pegged to a station's own throughput rather than a fixed subsidy, payback time scales directly with how much fuel actually moves through that station. A higher-throughput station recovers its investment faster than a lower-throughput one, by design.

The base-case numbers

In the documented base case, that works out to an estimated payback period of about 5.3 months and an estimated annual ROI of roughly 231% — both calculated directly from the assumptions shown on the ROI Calculator page, not confirmed market prices. CALCULATED — treat as a starting model to adjust

Treat this as a starting model

These figures are a starting point built on stated, adjustable assumptions — hardware cost, subscription fee, throughput, evaporation rate, capture efficiency, fuel price — not a guarantee. Open the ROI Calculator and substitute your own station's numbers to see your own payback estimate.
