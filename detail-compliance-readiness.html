Getting Ahead Of A Mandate That May Expand

Getting ahead of a regulatory direction that's been moving one way for years, before it becomes a deadline.

What's actually required today

The December 2021 NGT directive currently requires vapor recovery only for new petroleum outlets. Existing stations, including nearly all rural ones, face no mandate today. VERIFIED This isn't a compliance product in the sense of meeting a current legal requirement.

Where the direction has been heading

The regulatory direction over the past several years has consistently moved toward wider vapor recovery coverage, not narrower — new-outlet requirements are frequently how these mandates start, before eventually extending to existing infrastructure as the technology becomes more accessible.

Positioned ahead, not caught behind

A station that installs VaporGuard now isn't reacting to a mandate under time pressure — it's already positioned ahead of one, should the requirement ever be extended to existing outlets the same way it already has been for new ones.
