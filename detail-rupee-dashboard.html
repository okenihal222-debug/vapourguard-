Why We Lead With Rupees, Not Compliance

Why the dashboard is built to answer 'how much money' before it ever mentions compliance.

Compliance doesn't motivate an owner who isn't required to comply

Compliance-framed pitches (“this reduces your emissions”) tend to land as a cost, not a benefit — especially for an owner who isn't currently under any legal mandate to install anything at all. The NGT's December 2021 directive only requires vapor recovery for new outlets, so for the vast majority of existing small and rural stations, there's no regulation forcing this decision either way. VERIFIED — Dec 2021, CPCB/SPCB

Leading with the number that already matters

The dashboard instead leads with rupees, because recovered vapor is recovered, saleable fuel — and the dashboard converts sensor readings directly into an estimated daily and annual savings figure, calculated against that specific station's own throughput, not a generic industry average.

Nothing hidden behind the number

Every figure the dashboard shows is traceable back to a documented, adjustable assumption: throughput, evaporation rate, capture efficiency, fuel price. Open the ROI Calculator to change any of those assumptions yourself and watch the number respond — this isn't a black box.
