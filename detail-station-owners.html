The Problem — Deep Dive

Station Owners: Fuel They Already Paid For, Gone

The fuel that quietly disappears from the tank before anyone notices — and why nobody's had a number to put on it, until now.

An invisible line item

Evaporative loss isn't a rounding error. Industry technical literature puts it at roughly 0.18%–0.5% of total fuel throughput across both capture stages combined, and about two-thirds of that happens during tank refilling (Stage 1) alone — before a single customer has even been served that day. VERIFIED — industry literature

Money that's already been spent

This fuel isn't a discount or a future cost — the owner has already paid the dealer/OMC price for every litre sitting in that tank. Fuel lost to evaporation isn't insured, isn't reimbursed, and can't be recovered after the fact once it's gone. On a business that runs on thin per-litre commissions, that's a direct subtraction from margin, every single day.

Why it's stayed invisible

There's no meter on a standard station that reads “litres evaporated today.” Owners feel the effect — thinner margins than the maths of their sales should produce — without ever being able to point to the cause. That's precisely the gap VaporGuard's dashboard exists to close: not by reducing a loss owners can already see, but by making a loss they've never been able to see, visible and quantified.
