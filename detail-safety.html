Safety Is More Than The Headline Health Case

The health case gets the headline, but there's a second safety benefit that's just as real.

Beyond the health case

Lower vapor concentration at the nozzle and tank vents reduces attendant benzene exposure, as covered in depth on the Pump Attendants page. But reduced fuel-vapor concentration in the air around a dispensing point also lowers the immediate fire and flashpoint risk that comes with any accumulation of flammable vapor in a working environment.

A baseline expectation, unevenly met

This is a basic workplace-safety expectation at any modern fuel station — and one that small and rural sites currently have far less engineering support to meet than their urban counterparts, who've had Stage 2 vapor recovery systems in place for years.

Two risks, one fix

Health exposure and fire/flashpoint risk are usually discussed separately, but they share the exact same root cause — uncaptured vapor sitting in the air around the pump — so the same retrofit addresses both simultaneously.
