Fuel Saved At Scale

What one recovered litre a day turns into once you multiply it across a real market.

Starting at one station

At the single-station level, the base-case model estimates roughly 13.5 L of vapor captured per day rather than lost to the atmosphere — a modest number on its own, deliberately calculated from conservative assumptions rather than a best-case scenario. CALCULATED

Scaling the same math

That number scales roughly linearly with adoption: about 1,350 L/day across 100 stations, or approximately 392,000 L/day if adopted across all ~29,000 underserved rural stations in India. CALCULATED — engineering projection, not measured These are projections built on stated assumptions, not measurements from a fleet of already-deployed units — worth treating as directional, not exact.

Why this matters beyond emissions

Fuel is a resource India imports and prices at a national level. Reducing waste at the pump is a small but genuinely real contribution to reducing that import burden, entirely independent of the emissions case made elsewhere on this site.
