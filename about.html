Why this project exists

The mission, in one paragraph, and the numbers behind it.

India's fuel retail network has crossed 100,000 outlets, and nearly a third are rural — a segment the current vapor recovery mandate doesn't reach. VaporGuard exists to close that gap: protecting the people who work at these stations every day, and giving small station owners a financially self-justifying reason to adopt the same protection larger, newer stations already have.

The mission is simple: turn a mandatory pollution-control obligation into a savings story small station owners actually want to adopt.
