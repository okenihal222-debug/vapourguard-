The Mechanism Behind The Reduction

The direct link between one captured litre of vapor and the air quality right around the pump.

The mechanism, restated simply

Every litre of vapor captured instead of released is a litre of benzene and other VOCs that never reach ground level near the pump. This is the exact same physical process discussed on the Nearby Communities page — the difference here is looking at it from the mitigation side rather than the risk side.

A benchmark that already exists

Cities with vapor recovery adoption have documented an 84% cut in VOC emissions. VERIFIED — market research, 2025 That's not a projection — it's an already-observed outcome, one that's stayed concentrated in places with the infrastructure to install expensive Stage 1/Stage 2 systems.

Who's been excluded from that benchmark

Small and rural stations — roughly 29% of India's station network and growing — have been effectively excluded from that benchmark until now, simply on cost grounds rather than need. That's precisely the segment VaporGuard is built for.
